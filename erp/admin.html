Admin User Manual

Welcome to the Vinod Institute ERP Dashboard. Here is a quick guide on how to manage your daily operations.

1. Managing Students

  • Add a Student: Click the "Add Student" button, fill in their details (Name, Class, Join Date, Base Fee), and save. This registers them into the database.
  • Search: Use the search bar to quickly find a student by their name.
  • Delete: Click the trash icon next to a student to remove them from the system permanently.

2. Attendance Tracking

  • Marking Attendance: Select the Date and Class, then click "Load Students". Mark students as Present or Absent and click "Save Attendance".
  • Monthly Reports: Select a Month and Class, then click "View Report" to see a monthly grid. You can also download this as a CSV file.

3. Fee Management

  • Logging Payments: Select the fee month and filter by class. The system will load all students. You can mark fees as "Paid" or "Pending" and enter the exact amount collected.
  • CSV Export: Click the CSV button to download the fee collection data for your records.

4. Progress Reports

  • Adding Marks: Select a student from the dropdown on the left. Enter the test name, maximum marks, and obtained marks, then save.
  • Viewing Performance: Selecting a student will automatically load their test history on the right side, calculating their percentages.

5. Website Enquiries

  • Receiving Leads: Whenever a parent fills out the "Ready to Join Us" form on the main website, it instantly appears here.
  • Status Tracking: Click "Mark Contacted" once you call the lead. Click "Resolve" when they are enrolled or closed.
